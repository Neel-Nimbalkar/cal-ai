What you are agreeing to

When you check the consent box and then confirm through the email we send, you ask CalAI to send occasional waitlist updates and early-access invitations to the address you provided.

Double opt-in

  1. Submit the form with the consent box checked.
  2. Open the confirmation email and click the unique link within 7 days.
  3. Only then is your address marked confirmed.

Submitting the form without confirming does not enroll you in marketing email.

Your choices

Consent is optional and is not required to use the local calorie tracker. You may withdraw it at any time through an unsubscribe link in a future campaign email or by making a privacy request. We record the notice version and signup and confirmation times as evidence of your choice.

Frequency and content

Email frequency will vary during validation. Messages will concern CalAI development, research opportunities, product availability, and early access; we will not enroll you in unrelated third-party marketing.

Important

CalAI is a wellness tool, not a medical service. Do not reply with medical details or other sensitive information. See the Privacy Notice for data handling details.
