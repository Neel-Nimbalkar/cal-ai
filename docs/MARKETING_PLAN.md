# CalAI Marketing Strategy

## Goal and audience
Acquire 3,000 qualified waitlist members in 30 days, convert at least 35% into activated users, and reach 25% D7 retention before scaling paid acquisition. Target busy adults aged 25–40 who abandoned calorie trackers because logging took too long.

**Positioning:** The calorie tracker that does not feel like homework.

**Message:** Log a meal in five seconds. Take a photo, confirm the estimate, done. CalAI shows estimation confidence, makes correction easy, uses neutral language and never sells food or weight data.

## Funnel
Awareness → live no-account trial → segmented waitlist → target and first meal in under three minutes → reminders and weekly progress → five free photo scans/week → Pro at $9.99/month or $59.99/year → confirmed-referral rewards.

## Channel allocation
- **35% founder-led video:** four native TikTok/Reels/Shorts posts weekly using speed proof, AI transparency and founder-story formats.
- **25% micro-creators:** 15 tailored messages weekly to fitness, meal-prep and sustainable-weight creators with 5k–50k followers. Optimize for activated users and D7 retention, not views.
- **15% communities:** ten value-first answers for each disclosed product post; obey community rules and never mass-message.
- **15% search:** launch with TDEE, tracking-friction and photo-estimation accuracy articles, then publish twice monthly.
- **10% launch/email:** Product Hunt coming-soon in week three, Tuesday launch, one useful weekly email segmented by the waitlist friction answer.

## Weekly cadence
- Monday: product progress and funnel review.
- Tuesday: native demo and five creator messages.
- Wednesday: search content and community participation.
- Thursday: founder-story video and five creator messages.
- Friday: confidence/accuracy education and five creator messages.
- Weekend: meal-use-case video, user interviews and next week’s creative batch.

## Experiments
1. “Log a meal in five seconds” vs. “Tracking that doesn’t feel like homework.”
2. Product-led landing page vs. waitlist-first page.
3. Speed-comparison creator ad vs. confidence-transparency ad.
4. Free-month referral reward vs. priority access.
5. Target-first vs. first-meal-first onboarding.

Use at least 200 visitors per landing-page arm and define the primary metric before launch.

## Targets
| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---:|
| Awareness | 3-second video hold | 35% |
| Capture | Visit → waitlist | 15% |
| Trial | Visit → onboarding | 25% |
| Activation | Onboarding completion | 70% |
| Habit | Meals per active user/day | 2+ |
| Retention | D1 / D7 / D30 | 55% / 25% / 12% |
| Referral | Members with ≥1 referral | 20% |
| Revenue | Activated → trial | 8% |

## Budget
Start at $0 with founder content, community work and organic outreach. After message validation, allocate $500/month: $200 creator tests, $150 boosting proven native content, $50 referral tooling and $100 email/contingency. Move to $2,500/month only after D7 retention reaches 25%.

## Ethics
No before/after bodies, rapid-weight-loss promises, fake scarcity, BMI shaming, food morality or targeting minors. Disclose sponsorships, say estimates vary, and state that CalAI is not medical advice.
