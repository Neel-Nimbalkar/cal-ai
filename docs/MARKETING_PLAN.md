# CalAI waitlist and launch plan

## Positioning
**Log a meal in five seconds. Just take a photo.** CalAI is the calorie tracker for people who hate calorie trackers: visible estimation confidence, easy correction and no guilt language.

## 30-day motion
- Week 1: deploy waitlist; founder story; prototype video; Reddit pain-point research; first email.
- Week 2: confidence UI demo; build-in-public update; referral milestone email; five value-first community answers.
- Week 3: 15 tailored creator DMs; side-by-side logging speed test; Product Hunt coming-soon page.
- Week 4: seven-day countdown; one consented beta testimonial; launch Tuesday with native videos, waitlist email and honest community posts.

## Funnel targets
15% visit-to-waitlist initially, 70% onboarding completion, 35% waitlist activation, D1/D7/D30 retention of 55%/25%/12%, and organic CAC below $5. Do not scale paid acquisition until D7 retention reaches 25%.

## Ethics
No before/after bodies, fake scarcity, rapid-loss claims, food morality, BMI shaming or marketing to minors.
