Privacy Notice

Effective: September 19, 2026

This notice explains how CalAI handles information in this validation MVP.

Information we collect

  • Waitlist: email address, your selected tracking challenge, consent and confirmation timestamps, and subscription status.
  • Diary: calorie goals, food entries, and preferences remain in your browser’s local storage. CalAI does not receive them in this MVP.
  • Operational data: our hosting and email providers may process basic request, device, and delivery logs for security and reliability.

How we use information

We use waitlist data to confirm your request, send product updates and early-access invitations, understand broad user needs, prevent abuse, and operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use waitlist data for third-party advertising.

Legal basis and choices

We send waitlist email only after your consent and email confirmation. You may withdraw consent using the unsubscribe option in an email or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Sharing

Data may be processed by service providers that host the site, store waitlist records, and deliver email, solely to provide those services. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect the service and users.

Retention and security

Unconfirmed records and confirmation links are intended only to complete signup; confirmation links expire after 7 days. Confirmed waitlist data is retained until you unsubscribe, request deletion, or the waitlist closes, then deleted or anonymized within a reasonable operational period. We use access controls, one-way token hashing, expiring links, and encrypted transport, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and may complain to your data-protection authority. We will verify requests before acting.

Children and health information

The waitlist is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit medical records or sensitive health information. CalAI is not medical advice.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the project contact listed in the CalAI repository at github.com/Neel-Nimbalkar/cal-ai. A dedicated privacy email will replace this before broader launch.
